Availability

When I'm available

Because I run my own business during the week, I have a high degree of flexibility — and can often accommodate services at short notice.

  • Monday to Friday: daytime and evening bookings.
  • Saturdays: weddings, concerts and special services.
  • Sundays: afternoon and evening only — Choral Evensong, recitals and events. I'm not available on Sunday mornings because of my commitment as Organist and Choir Master at St John the Evangelist, Worsthorne.

Fees & booking

How fees and bookings work

  • Personal quotes. A short midweek service needs different preparation from a full concert, so every quote is based on the duration and complexity of what you need, and confirmed in writing before you book.
  • Deposits. For weddings, concerts and larger events, a deposit may be needed to secure the date. Balances are due before the event.
  • Rehearsals. Rehearsals with choirs, soloists or instrumentalists are charged at £80 per hour, agreed in advance.
  • Short notice. For services within 48 hours, please call or WhatsApp {{phone}} directly.
  • Confirmation. A booking is confirmed once we've agreed the fee and you receive written confirmation.
